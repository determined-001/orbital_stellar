## 1. What the backstop is

The backstop is a discretionary mutual arrangement funded by fees paid by
subscribers. Most subscribers will never need it; the pooled fund is used to fund
intervention for the minority of subscribers whose covered failure occurs. It is
mutual risk-sharing among participants, governed by these terms — it is **not** a
contract of insurance and does not transfer a defined risk to an insurer.

---

## 2. What we promise

1. **A published trigger.** Intervention is described by objective, published
   conditions set out in these terms and the associated product documentation.
2. **Pooling, not per-subscriber reserves.** Subscriber fees aggregate into a
   single fund used to fund intervention for covered failures.
3. **Discretionary operation within the rules.** Where the published conditions
   are met and pool capacity exists, the backstop acts according to its stated
   mechanics.
4. **Transparency.** The conditions, funding, and any limits are published and
   not changed retroactively to the disadvantage of a then-current subscriber
   except as permitted in §4.

---

## 3. What we do **not** promise

1. **Not insurance.** The backstop is not an insurance policy, cover note, or
   contract of indemnity. We are not an insurer, and subscribers are not
   policyholders or insured parties.
2. **No guarantee of outcome.** Meeting the trigger does not guarantee any
   particular result, intervention, or payout. Operation is discretionary and
   subject to pool capacity.
3. **No make-whole / indemnity.** We do not promise to compensate any subscriber
   for any loss, damage, or consequential harm, whether direct or indirect.
4. **No defined payout amount.** Nothing here fixes a payout size, ceiling, or
   timing. Any intervention is within available pool capacity.
5. **No risk transfer.** Subscribers do not transfer a defined risk to a regulated
   entity in exchange for consideration. Participation is voluntary and governed
   by these terms.
6. **No continuity guarantee.** The backstop may be modified, suspended, or
   discontinued for future periods per §4; no subscriber acquires a vested,
   permanent right to future intervention.
7. **No advice.** Nothing in these terms or any backstop material is financial,
   legal, tax, or regulatory advice.

## 4. Changes, suspension, and termination

- We may amend these terms for future periods on notice. Amendments do not apply
  retroactively to already-accrued, triggering failures except as required by law.
- We may suspend or discontinue the backstop on notice. Fees already paid for a
  period in which the backstop is active are governed by the terms in force for
  that period.
- Subscribers may stop participating; ceasing participation ends future
  obligations and future cover under these terms.

## 5. Availability and jurisdiction

- The backstop is offered only in jurisdictions where it is made available in
  writing. It is **not** offered in any jurisdiction where we have not confirmed
  availability.
- In-scope jurisdictions (preliminary): United States (per-state analysis
  required), United Kingdom, European Union, Switzerland, Singapore. All others are
  out of scope pending review. See the companion design document for the full
  considered / not-considered list.
- Nothing in these terms constitutes an offer where such an offer would be
  unlawful.
